Simple Rituals

Rituals are a way to focus your intention and connect with your practice. They don’t have to be complicated, especially when you’re just starting out.

Candle Ritual

A simple candle ritual can be done by lighting a candle and focusing on your intention or goal.

Journaling

Writing down your thoughts, intentions, or goals can be a simple and effective ritual.

Using Crystals

Holding or placing crystals nearby while focusing on an intention is another simple way to practice.

Witchcraft is often confused with Wicca, but they are not the same. Witchcraft is a practice, while Wicca is a specific religion.
