Simple Rituals

These beginner rituals are meant to be simple, safe, and focused on intention. They do not need to be complicated to feel meaningful.

Candle Ritual

This ritual is used for setting an intention or focusing on a goal.

Supplies

White candle, lighter, journal, quiet space

Steps

1. Sit somewhere quiet and take a few deep breaths.

2. Think about one intention or goal.

3. Light the candle carefully.

4. Focus on your intention while the candle burns.

5. Write down your thoughts in your journal.

6. Blow out the candle safely when finished.

Safety Note

Never leave a candle unattended.
